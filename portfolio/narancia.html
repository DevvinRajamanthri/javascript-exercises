Narancia Ghirga
Full name: Narancia Ghirga.
Age: 17.
Stand: Aerosmith — a small fighter plane Stand armed with machine guns and bombs.
Personality: loyal, loud, childish, but brave and kind-hearted.
Role: Member of Bucciarati’s team in Passione.
Abilities: Aerosmith can detect enemies by tracking their CO₂ and attack from long range.
Fate: Tragically dies near the end of Part 5.
