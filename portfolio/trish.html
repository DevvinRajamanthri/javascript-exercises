Trish Una
Full name: Trish Una.
Age: 15.
Role: Daughter of Diavolo, leader of Passione.
Stand: Spice Girl — softens any object, making it elastic and nearly indestructible.
Personality: starts spoiled and scared, but becomes brave and confident.
She fights alongside Bucciarati’s team and helps Giorno defeat Diavolo.
