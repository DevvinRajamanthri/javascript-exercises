Guido Mista
Full name: Guido Mista.
Age: 18.
Stand: Sex Pistols — six small bullet-shaped beings numbered 1–7 (skipping 4).
Each has its own personality and helps Mista control his gunfire.
Personality: carefree, confident, and loyal to his friends.
Fun fact: Mista has extreme superstition about the number 4.
