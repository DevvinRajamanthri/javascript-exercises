Giorno Giovanna
Full name: Giorno Giovanna (Japanese: ジョルノ・ジョバァーナ). Real name at birth: Haruno Shiobana. He is the son of DIO (using Jonathan Joestar’s body). Because of this, Giorno is both a Joestar and Dio’s son. Birthdate: April 16, 1985. Zodiac sign: Aries. Nationality: Japanese-British, but grew up in Italy. Age in Part 5: 15 years old. Personality: calm, determined, polite, but ruthless against evil. Dream: to become a “Gang-Star” and reform the mafia to protect the weak. Stand: Gold Experience → later evolves into Gold Experience Requiem. Gold Experience abilities: gives life to objects (turning them into animals/plants). Gold Experience Requiem ability: nullifies actions and returns them to “zero”, making Giorno practically unbeatable. At the end of Part 5, Giorno becomes the boss of Passione, the mafia organization.
